# SmartRes2

SmartRes2 coordinates party and raid recovery after a partial or total wipe. It helps resurrection-capable players choose useful targets, avoid duplicate casts, and see what is happening while the group recovers.

## Highlights

- A smart single-target resurrection keybind selects an eligible dead group member by role priority, then level, with random selection between equal candidates.
- Separate keybinds are available for smart single-target, manual-target, combat, and mass resurrection spells.
- Colour-coded bars distinguish active casts, competing casts, mass resurrection, and players waiting to accept a resurrection.
- Chat announcements and collision warnings can be configured, disabled, or personalized with custom random messages.

SmartRes2 includes extensive options for its bars, text, icons, messages, profiles, minimap button, and other presentation and behaviour settings without changing the underlying recovery logic.

## Supported World of Warcraft Versions

SmartRes2 supports:

- Retail
- Mists of Pandaria Classic
- Wrath Classic / Titan Reforged
- The Burning Crusade Classic
- Classic Era

Feature availability follows the capabilities of each game version. For example, mass resurrection options are shown only where mass resurrection spells exist.

## Configuration

Open SmartRes2 through Blizzard's AddOns settings, right-click its Broker or minimap button, or use one of these slash commands:

```text
/smartres2
/smartres
/sr
```

SmartRes2 keybindings are configured through Blizzard's Key Bindings interface.

## Translating

SmartRes2 can be localized through the [WowAce localization portal](https://www.wowace.com/projects/smartres2/localization).
